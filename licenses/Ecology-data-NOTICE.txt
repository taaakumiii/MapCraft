MapCraft processed geographic/statistical reference data

1. Active volcano positions
Source: Japan Meteorological Agency, List of Active Volcanoes in Japan
https://www.data.jma.go.jp/vois/data/filing/bulletin/catalog/appendix/v_active.html
Retrieved 2026-11-08. 111 representative volcano positions, converted from
degrees/minutes/seconds to decimal degrees. Additional named summits within a
single volcano are not separate volcano entries.
JMA website terms: https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/info/coment.html

2. Municipal population
Source: Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications, Basic Resident Register
Population, Demographic Changes and Households Survey, table 26-03 (total).
As of 2026-01-01; published 2026-07-29. Japanese and foreign residents combined.
https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/files?stat_infid=000040479049
Download: https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?fileKind=0&statInfId=000040479049
Retrieved 2026-11-08. Retained authority code, prefecture, municipality and total
population. City aggregate rows and designated-city wards remain distinct;
MapCraft selects the corresponding OSM municipal boundary without adding them.
e-Stat terms: https://www.e-stat.go.jp/terms-of-use

The above source data are not MapCraft's MIT source code. Preserve source
attribution and these transformation notes when redistributing.
Volcano influence radius, lava shape, depth, ore frequencies, population area
apportionment and villager counts are fictional gameplay adaptations.
They are not hazard maps, subsurface surveys, mineral-deposit predictions or
actual building occupancy information, and do not imply source endorsement.

Bedrock actor serialization was independently implemented with reference to
Microsoft's Actor Storage documentation and publicly observable data formats.
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/documents/actorstorage
Vanilla villager component group names were checked against Mojang's official
Bedrock Samples villager_v2 definition. No Mojang entity definition, artwork,
model, sound or texture is bundled by this feature.
